
 PathPilot: Hybrid AI Internship Recommendation System
PathPilot is a simple but surprisingly smart console application that helps students figure out which internships fit them best. You tell PathPilot your skills, experience level, and the field you're interested in — and it recommends roles that match you.
It uses a rule-based AI engine to score and rank internships, and a small offline “AI-like” advice generator to give you personal career suggestions. Everything runs locally, with no external API keys or internet required.
This makes PathPilot a perfect beginner-friendly AI project that still feels intelligent and useful.
________________________________________
 What PathPilot Can Do
•	Looks at your skills and interests, then suggests internships that make sense for you
•	Scores each internship based on how well it matches you
•	Gives you an optional “AI-generated” career advice section
•	Works completely offline — no hidden costs, no setup headaches
•	Easy to run, easy to customize, and great for learning how AI logic works
________________________________________
 How It Works (in simple words)
PathPilot uses two layers:
Rule-Based AI (the brain)
It checks how many of your skills match the internship, whether the domain matches your interest, your experience level, and even work mode (remote/onsite).
Then it gives each internship a score. Higher score = better match.
Offline AI Advice (the heart)
If you choose “yes”, PathPilot gives friendly, natural suggestions on:
•	What roles suit you
•	What skills you can improve
•	Why certain internships are a good match
It feels like a mentor giving you guidance.
________________________________________

 Why I Built This
Choosing an internship can feel overwhelming — especially when you're not sure which skills match which roles. PathPilot makes that process easier by breaking things down into simple logic and giving supportive guidance, not just a list of jobs.
It’s also a great learning project for AI concepts like rule-based systems and text generation.
